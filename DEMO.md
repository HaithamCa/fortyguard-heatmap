# Demo Video Script (2–5 minutes)

Record this as a screen + voiceover. Target **3 minutes**.

## Suggested structure

### 0:00–0:30 — Problem
> “Cities feel heat differently block by block. Averages hide dangerous hotspots.”

Show a quick city map / HeatSafe landing.

### 0:30–1:00 — Solution intro
> “HeatSafe uses FortyGuard’s Temperature API to analyze a neighborhood and guide people to cooler places.”

Click **Start Analysis**.

### 1:00–2:00 — Live workflow
1. Zoom to a **small US area** (e.g. Lower Manhattan)
2. Draw polygon (3+ points) → **Finish Polygon**
3. Set date/time (historical summer afternoon works well)
4. Click **Analyze area**
5. Narrate polling: “We submit to FortyGuard, then poll until tiles complete.”

### 2:00–3:00 — Results & impact
Show:
- Cool → hot colored tiles
- Min / Avg / Max metrics
- Temperature locations list
- Recommendation: “Go to the coolest spot”
- Click **Open in Google Maps**

Close with:
> “HeatSafe turns FortyGuard hyperlocal temperature data into safer decisions in seconds.”

## Recording tips
- Use a successful US AOI you already tested
- Keep the drawn area small to avoid long waits
- If a timeout appears briefly, keep recording — the app retries
- End on Google Maps open at the coolest location
